# DFGM paper campaign after expanded compute authorization

This is the prospective execution protocol. The user authorized as much
Discovery compute as needed, including beyond 64 CPUs, and asked for periodic

the earlier two-hour standing allowance for this task. It does not authorize

## Statistical protocol

- Run all twelve published n=200 settings with 100 paired replicates each.
  Use the existing recorded seed recipe, five-Fourier generator, 101-node
  integration and explicit overlapping-transition convention. Retain both
  the nominal dynamic precision and the actual projected-process precision.
  The latter is the primary statistical target; the former is used to assess
  agreement with the published tables. Author-code agreement is unverified.
- Retain all applicable original comparator families, whole-curve bandwidth
  CV over .1,.2,.3,.4, FDA AIC rank up to five and pointwise BIC. Keep DGM's
  proportionality constant one explicit; the authors do not supply an exact
  reproducible choice within their stated (0,3] range. This and other
  conventions can cause published-table discrepancies; report them.
- For each proposed ACLIME method, evaluate K2 on 31 logarithmic values from
  .03 to 2 and bias constants .01,.1,1,10,20. Choose the pair by observable
  pointwise BIC. This is a prespecified empirical tuning protocol, **not a
  claim that every selected constant is a valid theoretical bias envelope**.
  Earlier population diagnostics invalidated the default constant-one envelope.
  Known zero centering and known noise variance .5 are explicit simulation
  inputs for direct methods. Estimated-mean sensitivity remains additional
  evidence to develop, not an inherited theorem.
- To compute each direct method's ROC, first fix its bias constant using that
  observable BIC choice, then vary K2. Do not form a truth-optimized envelope
  over bias constants. Keep fitted endpoints, partial areas, bounds and
  undefined full AUROC distinct. Invalid positive-definiteness/BIC selections
  and failed candidates remain missing and counted.
- Retain default quantile scaled lasso and the separate six-penalty BIC path.
  The visit-adjusted candidate is empirical. Its smoothed, projected Gram
  matrix does not automatically inherit the iid scaled-lasso theory.
- Expand the sixteen known-target controls to 100 replicates per setting.
  Replay the same 1600 datasets for held-out scaled-lasso tuning. Keep the
  exact known bias envelope, 160/40 split, common K2 menu, all adverse defaults,
  failures, paired differences and Monte Carlo SE. Known-envelope controls
  do not demonstrate adaptation to unknown smoothness.

Full execution does not by itself establish replication of the published
numbers or superiority of a proposal. Report actual agreement and discrepancies.
Compute Monte Carlo SE across independent replicates, after averaging time
within each replicate. Compare methods on the same seeds and report paired
uncertainty. The earlier three-replicate results informed this protocol and
must remain labeled development evidence.

full bandwidth CV and the older exploratory constant-one calibration. Its

`639c0ada8e7809164cfd21f09454bb4a11869c70`. It is not pooled with production.
Discovery jobs **13412428,13412429,13412430** each use four CPUs, 8 GB and a
two-hour limit. The expanded bias-menu canary is job **13413022**. Source

The bias-menu canary passed 58 common and 43 irregular fits. Job **13413942**
validated output counts, BIC-to-ROC bias consistency, four-replicate control
and calibration loops, exact default replay, and backward-compatible summaries.
The largest four production settings (3,6,9,12), with all 31 path values and
five bias constants, run separately under job **13414315**, with 6 GB per
worker. This is a full-size representative check before the remaining
production pools are dispatched. Validated matching canary outputs can be
reused, while every intended dataset remains in the coverage manifest.

The older pilot pool **13412428** retained completed settings 1 and 7 but
lost settings 4 and 10 to the 1.8 GB worker limit. Those two missing datasets
are retried with identical science, 6 GB workers and `JULIA_HEAP_SIZE_HINT=4G`
under job **13414834**. The originals remain retained. Pending control pools
13414044/13414046 were cancelled before starting and replaced by eight-worker
jobs **13414835/13414836**, with a 1000 MiB heap hint, to reduce queue delay.
The latter runs after the control producer, preserving the paired datasets.
Heap hints change garbage-collection behavior, not statistical settings; see
the [Julia memory documentation](https://docs.julialang.org/en/v1.12/manual/memory-management/).

Production job sizing uses measured pilot stage times and memory, plus the
larger precision-path storage. For p=100,T=50 the 534 candidate matrices alone
occupy about 2.14 GB in Float64. Plan approximately 6 GB per worker rather
than extrapolating the reduced canary's 1.8 GB request. At 1200 datasets,
retaining paths, covariance inputs and reporting tables is expected to require
roughly 1–1.5 TB and tens of thousands of files. Project storage had 3.5 TB
free at setup. Recheck capacity before expanding retention; no cleanup is
automatic. Covariance inputs are serialized for expensive sensitivity work.

Use bounded GNU Parallel pools with exclusive `srun` steps and
single-threaded BLAS. The current implementation uses four Julia threads per
worker for independent time points and FPCA components; native solvers remain
single-threaded. The joint GGL objective is unchanged. Current normal QoS limits are 2000 CPUs and 100 running
jobs per user; main permits at most 48 hours per job. The initial CPU feature
set is `epyc-7513|epyc-7542`. Record actual nodes and memory. Adjust pool sizes,
features and wall limits when queue evidence supports it. Changes to scientific
settings require a separate recorded source/protocol variant, not a scheduling

## Monitoring and completion

A tested macOS launchd job (`edu.usc.mkolar.dfgm-monitor`) dispatches a
same-thread continuation every 30 minutes through `codex queue`. The prior
automation TOML had no execution receipt and is paused/superseded. The launchd
job is loaded and its first queue operation returned a receipt. Deduplication
allows one pending continuation; each resumed turn acknowledges it. This host
mechanism requires the Mac and Codex session to be available. Cluster workers
continue independently of the SSH connection.

Check exact recorded jobs, cheap progress counters, memory and errors. Reconcile
pending/running jobs before replacement; recover missing/invalid work only.
Notify meaningful failures or changes; preserve source variants and failures.

Completion requires explicit coverage and failure accounting for all 1200
paper datasets and 1600 controlled datasets, source/dependency correspondence,
selected-fit consistency, finite reported losses, paired uncertainty and an
honest published-table comparison. A completed scheduler job is insufficient.
After delivering the verified final report and updating the PR, pause the
heartbeat. Never mark the full task complete merely because the pilot ended.
